Rules and hand rankings

Everyone puts the boot in the pot and gets 3 cards face down. Going clockwise, each player bets or packs. The last player left, or the best hand at a show, takes the pot.

  1. Trail: three of a kind (A-A-A is best)
  2. Pure sequence: three in a row, same suit
  3. Sequence: three in a row, mixed suits
  4. Color: same suit, not in a row
  5. Pair: two of the same rank
  6. High card: none of the above

For sequences, A-K-Q is highest, A-2-3 is second, then K-Q-J and down to 2-3-4.

Blind players haven't looked at their cards. They bet the current stake, or double it to raise. Seen players bet 2× the stake (chaal), or 4× to raise. Every raise doubles the stake.

Side show: a seen player can pay a chaal to ask the previous seen player to compare cards privately. If they accept, the lower hand packs. On a tie, the player who asked packs.

Show: when only two players are left, either can pay to show. Both hands are revealed and the best wins. On a tie, the player who asked loses. If the pot hits the pot limit, everyone still in shows.

Turn timer: if the table has a timer and it runs out, that player packs automatically. An unanswered side show counts as refused. With no timer, anyone can pack for a player who's been away for 90 seconds.

When a round ends, everyone's cards are shown, including packed hands. This doesn't change who won. The pot was already decided.
